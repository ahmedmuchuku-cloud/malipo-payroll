Section 02

Financial Statement Presentation

ASC 210IAS 1
US GAAP — ASC 210

Liquidity-First Order

Assets and liabilities are presented in decreasing order of liquidity. Cash always leads, non-current assets appear last. Creditor-oriented and prescriptive.

IFRS — IAS 1

Reverse Liquidity Common

No format is mandated. Common practice presents non-current items first — PPE before cash. Reflects an owner/investor perspective on long-term assets.

Section 03

Inventory & Fixed Assets

ASC 330IAS 2 / IAS 16
The LIFO Conflict

The Single Biggest
Inventory Difference

LIFO is permitted and widely used under US GAAP to reduce taxable income during inflation. The LIFO Conformity Rule means if used for tax, it must also apply to financial statements.

IFRS strictly prohibits LIFO under IAS 2, creating material COGS and net income differences between the two frameworks.

Variable Consideration

US GAAP — ASC 606
Requires assessment of whether a "significant reversal" of cumulative revenue is unlikely to occur. Quantitative guidance in ASC 606-10-32-11.
IFRS 15 Approach
Focuses on whether it is "highly probable" no significant reversal will occur. "Highly probable" implies a higher confidence threshold in practice than "unlikely to reverse."
Licenses of Intellectual Property
Both distinguish "right to access" (over time) vs "right to use" (point in time). IFRS 15 provides slightly different indicators, leading to divergent outcomes for symbolic IP licences.

Collectability Thresholds

IFRS "probable" (~50%+) means revenue can be recognised earlier for transactions with credit risk than under typical US GAAP practice (~75-80%).

Section 05

Lease Accounting

ASC 842IFRS 16
US GAAP — ASC 842

Dual Classification Model

Lessee classifies leases as Operating or Finance. Operating leases show straight-line rent expense — no front-loading. Finance leases show interest + amortisation.

IFRS — IFRS 16

Single Finance Model

ALL leases treated as finance leases. P&L shows interest (declining) + depreciation = front-loaded total cost. EBITDA improves; early-year net income is lower.

Lease Liability Re-measurement

IFRS 16 — Frequent Triggers

Re-measure whenever there is a change in a rate or index (e.g. CPI, base rate) — creates P&L volatility in high-inflation environments.

ASC 842 — Limited Triggers

Re-measured only on lease modification or exercise/non-exercise of a purchase or extension option. More stable, but less reflective of economic changes.

Short-term Exemption (Both)

Optional exemption for leases ≤12 months. Under IFRS 16, an additional low-value asset exemption applies (~USD 5,000 new value) — no GAAP equivalent.

ASC 842 Lessee Classification

Key Insight: Under IFRS 16, the classification question is irrelevant — every lessee lease defaults to the finance model, simplifying accounting but amplifying EBITDA.
Section 06

Income Taxes

ASC 740IAS 12

Uncertain Tax Positions

Step 1 — US GAAP
Recognition

More-likely-than-not (>50%) position will be sustained on examination.

Step 2 — US GAAP
Measurement

Largest amount with >50% probability of being realised.

IFRS — IAS 12 + IFRIC 23

Single step: recognise using expected value (probability-weighted) or most likely amount, whichever better predicts resolution. No separate recognition threshold.

Tax Rate for DTAs

IFRS permits substantively enacted rates — a bill awaiting final assent. US GAAP requires full enactment only.

Intercompany Profits: IFRS uses the buyer's tax rate for consolidation eliminations. US GAAP uses the seller's rate — directly opposite, with material impact in cross-border groups.
Section 07

Financial Instruments

ASC 815 / CECLIFRS 9

IFRS 9 Classification

Classification driven by the entity's Business Model and whether cash flows are Solely Payments of Principal & Interest (SPPI).

US GAAP — CECL Model

The Current Expected Credit Loss model (ASC 326) requires immediate recognition of lifetime expected losses from Day 1 — regardless of whether a credit event has occurred.

Hedge Accounting: IFRS 9 allows a wider population of hedging instruments and hedged items than ASC 815. IFRS 9 also provides an option to continue applying IAS 39 hedge accounting — a transitional accommodation with no US GAAP equivalent.

Under which standard is LIFO inventory accounting strictly prohibited?

👆 Click card to reveal answer
Answer
IFRS (IAS 2)LIFO is strictly prohibited under IFRS. Only FIFO and Weighted Average Cost are permitted. US GAAP allows FIFO, LIFO, and Average Cost — subject to the LIFO conformity rule for tax purposes.
